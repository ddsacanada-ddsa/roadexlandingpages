Account and associated data deletion

You can permanently delete your Roadex account and the cloud data associated with it directly in the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Roadex Street Grarage.
  2. Open Membership / Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and all data.
  4. Read the confirmation and confirm permanent deletion.

You must be signed in to the account you want to delete. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app

Use the developer support email shown in the Developer contact section of the Roadex Street Grarage Google Play listing. Use the subject Roadex account deletion request and send the request from the Google email address connected to your Roadex account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deletion.

Data that is deleted

  • Your Supabase authentication account and linked Google identity record.
  • Server-side AI recognition and usage-limit records associated with your account.
  • Challenge invitations, participation records, results, scores, and cloud challenge metadata associated with your account.
  • Temporary challenge images still stored in Roadex cloud storage.

Data on your phone

Garage cards, ordinary car photos, preferences, and other local app data are stored on your device. Account deletion attempts to clear Roadex data on the current device. You can also remove remaining local data by clearing Roadex storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Copies you exported or shared outside Roadex must be deleted from those destinations separately.

Retention and exceptions

In-app deletion is processed immediately, although removal from encrypted backups and service-provider systems may take up to 30 days. Temporary challenge images are normally deleted after 15 days. We may retain limited records longer when required for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or completed financial transactions. Any retained data is restricted to those purposes and removed when it is no longer required.

Need help?

Contact DDSA Canada using the developer support email displayed on the Roadex Street Grarage Google Play listing. Include only the information needed to locate your account; never send your password or Google verification code.
