Privacy Policy

Roadex mobile application · Package app.spotryx.collect

Effective August 7, 2026

Roadex, developed and operated by DDSA Canada, turns photos of real-world vehicles into AI-assisted identifications and collectible cards. Some development builds may display the former internal name “StreetGarage.” This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

1. Information we access or collect

Photos and camera content

When you choose to photograph a vehicle or select an image from your library, Roadex accesses that image only after you grant the relevant permission or make a selection. A photo may incidentally contain people, licence plates, addresses, private property, or location metadata. Please avoid submitting images that contain information you do not have the right to process.

For live AI recognition and optional AI studio rendering, the selected image is transmitted from your device to Roadex’s backend and then to an AI service provider. Roadex does not intentionally save the submitted source image in its Supabase database. Service providers may temporarily process or retain request data under their applicable terms, security procedures, and legal obligations.

Anonymous account and authentication data

The current build creates an anonymous Supabase authentication account. This produces a random user identifier and session credentials; it does not require your name, email address, phone number, or social-media account. Session credentials are stored using secure device storage. Network providers may also process standard connection data such as IP address, request time, and device or application information for delivery, security, and abuse prevention.

AI usage and diagnostic data

Roadex records operational metadata associated with AI requests, including a capture-cycle identifier, anonymous user identifier, AI feature and model used, request identifier, success or failure status, token or image usage counts, processing duration, estimated service cost, and error codes. This information helps us operate, secure, meter, diagnose, and improve the service. It is not used for third-party advertising.

Garage and collection data stored on your device

Roadex stores your saved vehicle cards and related details locally on your device. This can include the original and rendered image file locations, detected make and model, estimated year range, body style, colour, confidence score, rarity, visual evidence, alternatives, capture date, rendering choices, and capture-cycle identifier. Current builds do not provide cloud synchronization of your personal garage.

Location, contacts, microphone, and advertising

Roadex does not request precise or approximate device-location permission, access your contacts, or record audio. The current closed-testing build does not include third-party advertising or advertising-profile SDKs. A service provider may infer a general area from an IP address for network security and service delivery.

2. How we use information

  • Provide vehicle identification, confidence-aware results, and optional AI-rendered card images.
  • Create and maintain your on-device garage and capture history.
  • Authenticate requests and protect the service from misuse, fraud, and excessive automated use.
  • Measure AI-service usage and cost, diagnose failures, and maintain performance and reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and security of users, Roadex, and service providers.

3. Service providers and disclosures

Roadex uses service providers that process information on our behalf to deliver the app’s core functionality:

  • Supabase: anonymous authentication, backend functions, database hosting for operational usage records, and related infrastructure.
  • OpenAI: AI-based vehicle identification and optional image transformation. Submitted vehicle images and instructions are processed through OpenAI’s API services.
  • Google and device-platform services: distribution through Google Play and on-device components used by the Android application. Google Play may independently process download, device, purchase, crash, and diagnostic information under Google’s terms.

We may also disclose information where required by law; to investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents; to protect legal rights or safety; or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of the Roadex business, subject to appropriate safeguards.

4. Data retention and deletion

On-device garage data remains on your device until you remove it using available app controls, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Deleting an item from the Roadex database may not delete a separate copy you previously exported or saved outside the app.

Anonymous account records and AI usage metadata are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide and secure the service, manage costs, investigate errors or abuse, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. We may aggregate or de-identify operational information so it no longer reasonably identifies a user, and retain that de-identified information for service analysis.

You may request deletion of the anonymous account and associated server-side usage records by using the developer support contact displayed on the Roadex Google Play listing. Include the words “Roadex data deletion request” and, if available, the anonymous account or capture identifier shown by the app. We may retain limited records where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or proof that a deletion request was completed.

5. Security

Roadex uses encrypted HTTPS connections for data sent from the app, secure device storage for authentication credentials, access controls, and database row-level security. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device protected and do not submit highly sensitive content.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Camera and photo permissions: deny or revoke access through Android settings. You can still use features that do not require those permissions.
  • AI submission: do not capture or select a photo if you do not want it transmitted for AI processing.
  • Local data: use available deletion controls, clear app storage, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored Roadex data.
  • Privacy requests: use the developer support contact on the Roadex Google Play listing to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of certain personal information. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and may need information to verify and locate the relevant anonymous record.

7. Children’s privacy

Roadex is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users who are minors where they live should use the app only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child submitted personal information, contact us through the developer support details on the Google Play listing so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

8. International processing

Roadex and its service providers may process information in Canada, the United States, and other countries where they operate. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from those in your country. Where required, we use appropriate contractual or legal safeguards for international transfers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Roadex changes. We will post the revised policy at this publicly accessible page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or through the Google Play listing when appropriate.
